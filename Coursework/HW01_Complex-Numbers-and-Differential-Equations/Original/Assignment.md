This is the first assignment of Neural Network Control Systems. It covers complex numbers and differential equations from Week 2: complex numbers are written and computed in two forms, and differential equations with constant coefficients are solved.

---

<br>

## Problem 1. From Polar Form to Cartesian Form (15 points)

Convert the complex numbers given in polar form below into Cartesian form.

- (1) $2\angle(-45^\circ)$
- (2) $1\angle 90^\circ$
- (3) $1\angle 180^\circ$

---

<br>

## Problem 2. From Cartesian Form to Polar Form (15 points)

Convert the complex numbers given in Cartesian form below into polar form.

- (1) $\sqrt{3} - j$
- (2) $-1 - j$
- (3) $-j$

---

<br>

## Problem 3. Subtraction of Complex Numbers and the Complex Plane (10 points each)

Compute the difference $S_1 - S_2$ of the two given complex numbers, draw the result in the complex plane formed by the real and imaginary axes, and check that the drawing agrees with the computed value.

- (1) $S_1 = 2 + j$, $S_2 = 1 - j$
- (2) $S_1 = 2 + j$, $S_2 = -1 + j$

---

<br>

## Problem 4. Multiplication and Division of Complex Numbers (20 points)

Perform the following computations for the two given complex numbers $S_1 = 1\angle 120^\circ$ and $S_2 = 2\angle 30^\circ$.

- (1) (10 points) Compute the product and the quotient using Cartesian form.
- (2) (10 points) Compute the product and the quotient using polar form.

---

<br>

## Problem 5. Differential Equations (15 points each)

Solve the following differential equations.

- (1) $\dot{y} + 2y = 3$, $y(0) = 1$
- (2) $\ddot{y} + 2\dot{y} + 2y = 1$, $y(0) = 1$, $\dot{y}(0) = 0$
