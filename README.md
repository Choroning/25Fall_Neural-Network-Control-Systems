## 📝 About This Repository

- `Lecture-Notes/` holds weekly lecture notes (`Week01`, `Week02`, and so on), each covering the three lectures of that week.
- `Coursework/` holds each assignment's distributed handout, the submitted solution, and an assignment explanation that covers the concepts and the solution steps of every problem.
- Every lecture note and explanation document comes as an English (`.md`) and Korean (`.ko.md`) pair.

> **🤖 AI Agent Usage**
> [Claude Code](https://claude.ai/download) was used to organize and translate the lecture notes and assignment explanations.

- **Affiliation:** Korea University Sejong

| Course&nbsp;Code| Course            | Type          | Instructor      | Department                              |
|:----------:|:------------------|:-------------:|:---------------:|:----------------------------------------|
|`DAMO335-00`|NEURAL NETWORK CONTROL SYSTEMS|Major Elective|Prof. Unggi&nbsp;Lee|Department of Autonomous Mobility|

### Course Overview

A major elective course on the modeling of engineering systems, including mechanical and electrical systems, and on control methods based on artificial neural networks, covering the analysis and performance of feedback control systems, PID control, the basics of artificial intelligence and neural networks, and neural network controllers. The instructor, schedule and class format (video lectures), assessment, course policies, and progress are in the [course information document](Course-Information.md).
